Wildhaven Zoo privacy policy

Applies to the Wildhaven Zoo app and its online service (sign-in, cloud saves and Versus matches).

Playing without an account

You can play Wildhaven Zoo without signing in. Then nothing leaves your device: your zoo is saved only on the phone or tablet.

What we store when a grown-up signs in with Google

  • Google account id (a number Google gives us) and the email address, only when Google says it is verified. We use the email to show "Signed in as" and to answer support questions.
  • Players: the nickname and the animal picture chosen for each player. Nicknames are filtered, and we ask for a nickname, not a real name.
  • Zoo saves: the game data of each player's zoo, with the day, money, stars, number of animals and play time shown on the save cards.
  • Device labels: a random install id the app makes, the device model name (for example "Pixel 7") and the app version, so the app can say which device a zoo was last played on.
  • Versus matches: the match settings, each player's points over the match, and the final zoo save (kept for 30 days to check fair play).

What we do not store

  • No Google name, profile picture, contacts or location.
  • No IP addresses in our database, and no advertising or analytics ids.
  • No chat and no free text other than player nicknames.

Who can see what

  • Only the signed-in Google account can see its players and zoos.
  • In a Versus match the other player sees only your player's nickname, animal picture and points. They never see an email address, an account id or a device name.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone. Data is stored on one server in the EU and sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

How long we keep it

  • Older copies of a zoo are kept for up to 30 days so a lost or replaced zoo can be brought back.
  • A removed player can be restored for 30 days, then it is deleted with all its zoos.
  • Finished Versus matches are deleted after 180 days.
  • Deleting the account deletes everything at once. Server backups that still contain it are replaced within 14 days.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings › Account › Delete account. You can also ask us by email; see how to delete your account.
