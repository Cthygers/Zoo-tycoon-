Delete your Wildhaven Zoo account

Wildhaven Zoo accounts are made by signing in with Google. Deleting the account removes every player, every zoo saved online and the sign-in itself.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Wildhaven Zoo and go to Settings › Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and answer the grown-up question.
  3. Sign in with the same Google account once more to confirm.

The account is deleted straight away. Zoos that were only on this phone stay on the phone until you uninstall the app.

Ask us to delete it

If you cannot use the app, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the Google address you signed in with and ask us to delete the Wildhaven account. We do it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted at once: the account, the email address, every player (nickname and picture), every online zoo save and older copy, and all sign-ins on every device.
  • Versus matches: in a match against someone else your player is shown as "Player left"; the other player keeps their own points. Matches with nobody left are deleted.
  • Backups: server backups that still contain the account are replaced within 14 days.

To remove only one player instead, use Manage players › Remove; a removed player can be restored for 30 days. See the privacy policy.
